Welcome to the official blog for **Sourcier**!

## What is Sourcier?

Compact and efficient source file mapping and position tracking for Rust: register files once, pack every span into a single u64.

## What to expect here

This blog is the central hub for everything related to Sourcier. We will be publishing:

- **Deep dives** into the architecture, design decisions, and inner workings of the project.
- **Tutorials and how-to guides** to help you get up and running quickly.
- **Release notes** and feature announcements as the project evolves.

Whether you are just discovering Sourcier or have been using it for a while, we are glad you are here. Stay tuned for our first official deep dive!
